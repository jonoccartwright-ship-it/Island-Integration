Estate Wi-Fi & networking

Estate Wi-Fi and whole-home networking for luxury residences in the Bahamas

Not an internet provider and not an IT shop: we design the network inside the property. Full-speed coverage in every room, the guest house, the terrace and the dock, built to carry automation, cameras, streaming and work-from-home without a single dead zone.

What we do

Internet providers deliver a connection to the wall and IT companies look after offices. Neither designs a network for a large home, and that gap is why so many beautiful houses in Nassau have Wi-Fi that drops in the master bedroom and dies on the terrace. A residence with automation, cameras, streaming in every room, video calls and a guest cottage is a small campus, and it needs to be designed like one.

Island Integration designs and installs whole-home and estate networks for residences across New Providence and the Family Islands: wired access points placed for the construction and floor plan, managed switching, structured cabling to every room, fiber between buildings, failover between two internet services where uptime matters, and remote monitoring so problems are found before you notice them. We work with Araknis and other professional-grade equipment from Snap One, supported through OvrC remote management. For businesses, we also design networks for offices, boutique hospitality and retail spaces.

What’s included

  • Wi-Fi design. Access point placement planned for your floor plan and construction, with wired backhaul to each one.
  • Managed switching and routing. Enterprise-grade routers and switches sized for cameras, streaming and automation traffic.
  • Structured cabling. Cat6 or better to every room and device location, plus fiber between buildings and over long runs. See design, cabling and racks.
  • Outdoor coverage. Weather-rated access points for pools, terraces, guest cottages and docks.
  • Guest and device networks. Separate networks for guests, staff and smart devices to keep your main network secure.
  • Remote monitoring. Alerts and remote diagnostics through OvrC, so most issues are fixed without a visit.
  • Backup power. UPS protection so the network stays up through brief outages and generator transfers.

How we work

How we approach estate Wi-Fi and home networking

  1. Survey the property

    We review plans or walk the site to understand construction, distances and where people actually use devices. Concrete, stone and metal roofs all change the design.

  2. Design for the devices you have and will have

    Today's home may have fifty or more connected devices. We size the network for that and for what is coming.

  3. Install, label, document

    Clean cabling, a labeled patch panel and a ventilated rack. Every device and address documented so support is fast.

  4. Monitor and maintain

    Firmware, security updates and performance are monitored remotely as part of our support plans.

Designed for the islands

Good to know in the Bahamas

  • Concrete-block construction. Signals do not pass through block and reinforced walls the way they do through timber-frame homes. Wired access points in the right locations are the answer, as explained in why Wi-Fi struggles in concrete homes.
  • Salt air. Outdoor equipment needs proper enclosures and corrosion-resistant hardware, and indoor racks need conditioned air.
  • Power. Outages and surges damage networking equipment more than anything else. Surge protection and UPS are standard in our designs.
  • Internet options. We can design for fiber, cable, fixed wireless or satellite service, including automatic failover between two connections for properties that cannot be offline.

Frequently asked questions

Why not just use a mesh system from a store?

Consumer mesh relies on wireless links between units, which fail through concrete. Wired access points deliver full speed everywhere and can be monitored and managed. We explain the difference in how a wireless access point works.

Do you install networks for businesses?

Yes. We design and install networks for offices, boutique hospitality and retail spaces in Nassau, including guest Wi-Fi and secure staff networks.

Can you fix my existing network?

Usually. We start with an assessment, and often the fix is a few correctly placed wired access points and a proper switch rather than a full replacement.
