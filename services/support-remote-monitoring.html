Support & remote monitoring

Smart home support and remote monitoring in the Bahamas

Technology needs care over time. We monitor networks and systems remotely, fix most issues without a visit, and keep software, security and equipment current as your needs change.

Request a consultation Call +1 (242) 820-9013

What we do

A smart home is a living system. Software updates arrive, internet providers change equipment, a power cut leaves a device confused, and a family's needs shift over the years. The difference between a system that stays a pleasure and one that slowly becomes an irritation is support.

We support every system we install and many installed by others. Remote monitoring lets us see problems, often before you do, and resolve most of them from our office. When a visit is needed, we arrive knowing what the issue is.

What’s included

  • Remote monitoring. Network and device health monitored through OvrC and manufacturer platforms, with alerts for outages and faults.
  • Remote resolution. Reboots, configuration changes and software fixes done remotely with your permission, usually within the same day.
  • On-site service. Scheduled and priority visits for hardware issues, with the diagnosis already done.
  • Updates and security. Firmware and software kept current, and network security reviewed so your cameras and devices stay protected.
  • Seasonal checks. Pre-hurricane-season reviews of backup power, surge protection and camera coverage.
  • Upgrades and additions. Adding rooms, replacing ageing equipment and extending systems as your property evolves.

How we work

How we approach support and remote monitoring

  1. Document the system

    Support starts with knowing what is installed. For systems we did not install, the first step is an assessment and a documented drawing of what is there.

  2. Monitor proactively

    Alerts for offline devices, failing links and power events reach us directly. Many issues are fixed before anyone in the house notices.

  3. Respond clearly

    You get a plain explanation of what happened and what was done, not a jargon-filled ticket.

Designed for the islands

Good to know in the Bahamas

  • Absentee owners. We can act as the local technical contact for owners abroad, coordinating with property managers and caretakers.
  • Power events. Outages and generator transfers are the most common cause of calls. Our backup power guide explains how we design around them, and monitoring tells us when a device has not recovered.
  • Hurricane season. Before the season we check backup power, confirm remote access works and review camera coverage. After a storm we help bring systems back online in the right order.

Frequently asked questions

Do you support systems you did not install?

Yes, in most cases. We begin with an assessment to understand what is installed and in what condition, then recommend a support approach.

Do I need an ongoing plan?

Not always, but monitored systems have fewer and shorter outages, and most of our clients prefer a plan to ad-hoc calls. We will recommend what suits your property.

Can you fix things while I'm abroad?

That is one of the main reasons for remote monitoring. With secure remote access we resolve most software and configuration issues without anyone needing to be at the property.
