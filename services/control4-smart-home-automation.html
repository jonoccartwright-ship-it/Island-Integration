Smart home automation

Control4 smart home automation in Nassau and across the Bahamas

One system for lighting, climate, shades, entertainment, cameras and door locks, controlled from a keypad, touch screen or your phone. We design, program and support Control4 for homes, villas and boutique commercial spaces.

What we do

A smart home should feel simple. Press Welcome and the lights come up, the shades rise, the air conditioning settles to your preferred temperature and your music starts in the kitchen. Press Goodnight and the house closes down, doors lock and the cameras arm. That is what a well-designed Control4 system does: it takes dozens of separate devices and gives you one calm way to live with them.

Island Integration is a Control4 integrator based in Nassau. We have designed and installed automation systems across New Providence, from apartments at Ocean Club and Paradise Island to estates in Lyford Cay, Old Fort Bay and Albany, and we support them long after the installation is finished.

What’s included

  • System design. We map every room, device and routine before anything is ordered, so the system fits the way you actually use the property.
  • Control4 controllers and interfaces. Core controllers sized to the home, with keypads, touch screens, remotes and the Control4 app on your phone.
  • Lighting, shades and climate. Integration with lighting control, motorized shades and thermostats so scenes can control the whole room.
  • Entertainment. TVs, streaming, multi-room audio and cinema rooms on one remote, with sources shared between rooms.
  • Security and access. Cameras, door locks, gates and intercoms visible and controllable from the same interface.
  • Programming and scenes. Custom scenes, schedules and automations written for your household, then refined after you have lived with them.
  • Remote management. Secure remote access so we can diagnose and fix most issues without a site visit.

How we work

How we approach control4 smart home automation

  1. Start with how you live

    We begin with a walk-through or a review of your plans. We ask what you do in each room at different times of day, who in the household uses what, and where the friction is today. That conversation decides which rooms get keypads, where touch screens go and which scenes matter most.

  2. Build on a solid network

    Every Control4 system depends on the network underneath it. We install enterprise-grade networking as part of the project, not as an afterthought, because a slow or unreliable network is the most common cause of a frustrating smart home.

  3. Install cleanly, document everything

    Equipment lives in a properly ventilated rack with labeled cabling and documented IP addresses. When something needs attention years from now, the system is understandable rather than a mystery box.

  4. Program, hand over, refine

    We program the system, walk you through it room by room, and then adjust scenes and schedules after you have used it for a few weeks. The second visit is where a good system becomes a great one.

Designed for the islands

Good to know in the Bahamas

  • Power and storms. Automation hardware needs clean, protected power. We specify surge protection and UPS backup for controllers and the network so brief outages do not leave the house dark or the cameras offline. See our guide to keeping systems running in an outage.
  • Second homes. Many of our clients spend part of the year abroad. Control4 lets you check cameras, adjust climate to control humidity, and let in a caretaker or guest from your phone, wherever you are.
  • Concrete construction. Thick block walls block wireless signals. We design wired backbones and place access points correctly so keypads, touch screens and streaming devices stay connected.

Frequently asked questions

Can Control4 be added to an existing home?

Yes. Retrofits are common. Wireless lighting, battery shades and a wired network backbone let us add automation without opening every wall. New construction gives the most flexibility, so if you are building, read our pre-wire checklist early.

Does the system work if the internet goes down?

Local control continues to work without the internet. Lights, shades, audio and scenes run on the controller inside the home. Remote access from outside the property and cloud streaming services need a connection.

Is Control4 difficult to use?

The goal is the opposite. A well-programmed system reduces a wall of switches and a pile of remotes to a few clear buttons. Guests and family members can use it without a tutorial.

How does Control4 compare with DIY smart devices?

DIY products are good at single jobs. Control4 is designed to tie many systems together reliably and to be supported by a professional. We compare the two approaches in this article.
