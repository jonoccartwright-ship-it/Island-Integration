Lighting control

Lighting control for homes in Nassau and the Bahamas

Replace banks of switches with engraved keypads, set scenes for every moment of the day, and let the house follow the sun. We design and install Lutron and Control4 lighting control for new builds and renovations.

What we do

Lighting shapes how a home feels more than any other system. The same room can be bright and practical at breakfast, soft and layered at dinner and barely lit on the way to bed. Lighting control makes those moods one button each, and it does it consistently every day without anyone walking around adjusting dimmers.

We design lighting control around the architecture and the way each space is used. That means fewer, better-placed keypads, scenes that make sense, and a system that your family and guests understand immediately.

What’s included

  • Lutron and Control4 lighting systems. Centralized or wireless dimming to suit new construction and retrofits.
  • Engraved keypads. Clean, labeled buttons such as Entertain, Dine and Off, matched to your finishes.
  • Scenes and schedules. Daylight-aware schedules, astronomic timers for exterior lighting and vacation modes that make the home look occupied.
  • LED compatibility. Dimmers matched to your fixtures so LEDs dim smoothly without flicker or buzz.
  • Landscape and pool lighting. Exterior zones on the same keypads and schedules as the interior.
  • Integration. Lighting scenes that also move shades, adjust climate and work with your Control4 system.

How we work

How we approach lighting control

  1. Lighting design before wiring

    We review the lighting plan with your architect or electrician and decide which circuits are dimmed, grouped or switched. Getting the load schedule right early avoids rework later.

  2. Keypad placement that makes sense

    One keypad at the entry to each room usually replaces three or four switches. We locate keypads where you naturally reach, and we keep the button labels simple.

  3. Commissioning and tuning

    After installation we set trim levels, fade rates and scene levels room by room, then adjust them again after you have lived with the system for a few weeks.

Designed for the islands

Good to know in the Bahamas

  • Heat and humidity. Dimmer panels and processors belong in a cool, dry, ventilated location. We plan that space with the electrical rooms rather than tucking equipment into a hot closet.
  • Exterior lighting. Salt air is hard on fixtures and connections. We specify marine-grade or coastal-rated exterior components and seal every connection.
  • Power quality. Surge protection on lighting processors and panels protects them from the spikes that come with grid switching and generator transfers.

Frequently asked questions

Can lighting control be installed in an existing home?

Yes. Wireless Lutron and Control4 dimmers replace existing switches without new wiring, and keypads can be added where a switch exists today. New builds allow centralized panels, which give the cleanest walls.

Will my existing LED fixtures dim properly?

Most will, provided the dimmer is matched to the fixture. We test fixtures before specifying dimmers and recommend compatible lamps where needed.

Do I still have normal switches?

Keypads replace switches, and every room still has a simple on and off. You never lose basic control.
