Why Island Integration

Technology should enhance the architecture, not fight it.

We are a Nassau-based smart home integrator. We design, install and support the systems that make a property easier to live in: control, lighting, networks, entertainment and security, all matched to the finish and layout of each space.

  • Design first. Cable routes, racks and equipment are planned early, so technology stays out of sight.
  • Reliability matters. Enterprise-grade networks are the backbone of every system we install.
  • One-touch living. Scenes turn complex spaces into simple experiences.
  • Long-term support. We maintain, monitor and evolve systems as your needs change.

Services

Smart home installation, from the first drawing to ongoing support

Every system is designed around the property, the people who use it and the way they live.

Control4 smart home automation

One system ties lighting, entertainment, climate, shades, security and cameras together, so the whole property is controlled from a touch screen, keypad or app. We design, program and support Control4 systems for homes and light commercial spaces in the Bahamas.

Read how a smart home works

  • Lighting control

    Lutron and Control4 lighting control: scene-based lighting, daylight automation and engraved keypads that make every room feel effortless.

  • Home cinema

    Home cinema design and installation: high-end projectors and screens, immersive audio, thoughtful seating layouts and lighting that sets the scene.

  • Automated shades

    Motorized window shades integrated with lighting and climate for comfort, privacy and efficiency.

  • Climate control

    Smart thermostats and HVAC integration for comfort and energy savings.

  • Multi-room audio & video

    Whole-home audio and TV distribution, including Sonos integration, so entertainment follows you from room to room. Audio visual installation for homes and boutique commercial spaces.

  • Professional Wi-Fi & networking

    Enterprise-grade Wi-Fi design and installation, fiber and resilient network infrastructure for fast, reliable connectivity indoors and out.

  • Security, access control & intercom

    Security camera installation, access control and intercom systems that give you visibility and peace of mind, from your phone or wall panel.

  • Design, cabling & equipment racks

    System design, structured cabling and technology rack layouts, planned with your architect or builder before the walls close.

  • Support & remote monitoring

    Ongoing support, remote monitoring and upgrades to keep everything running smoothly.
