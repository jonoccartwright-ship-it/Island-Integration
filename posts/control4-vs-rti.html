Control4 vs RTI: Which Control System Fits Your Home?

Both put the whole home on one remote, keypad or app, and both are installed and programmed by authorized dealers. They take different routes to get there. As a dealer for both, here is how we help clients choose.

By Island IntegrationNov 16, 20263 min read

Clients often arrive having heard of one system from a friend or a previous home. The honest answer to “which is better?” is that a well-programmed system of either kind beats a poorly programmed one of the other. The differences matter at the edges, and they are worth understanding before you commit.

What they have in common

  • One interface for entertainment, lighting, climate, shades, cameras and door locks.
  • Handheld remotes, in-wall touchpanels, keypads and a phone app that all show the same controls.
  • Local control. Lights, music and scenes keep working when the internet is down.
  • Dealer programming. Neither is a do-it-yourself product, and both depend on the quality of the programmer and the network underneath.

Where Control4 is strong

Control4 is designed as a whole-home platform. Its interface is consistent from room to room, which makes it easy for guests and family to learn, and it has its own lighting, keypads, thermostats and audio alongside support for a very wide range of other brands. Homeowners can make some changes themselves, such as adjusting scenes and schedules. It suits larger homes where many subsystems need to work as one, and new builds where lighting control and distributed audio are being designed together.

Where RTI is strong

RTI gives the programmer a blank canvas. Every screen and button can be designed for your equipment and the way you use it, so the remote does exactly what you want and nothing you do not. RTI is well known for its handheld remotes, and it scales comfortably from a single media room to a whole property. It is a strong choice when entertainment is the centre of the system, when a client wants a very specific interface, and in commercial spaces such as meeting rooms and bars.

Day-to-day differences

The remote. Both offer excellent handheld remotes. Control4 remotes show the same menus in every room. An RTI remote shows whatever was designed for that room, which can be as few as five buttons for a guest bedroom or a detailed layout for a cinema.

Making changes. With Control4, a homeowner can adjust some lighting scenes and schedules without calling anyone. With RTI, changes go through the dealer, and in return the layout stays exactly as designed. In both cases, adding or replacing equipment such as a new television or cable box needs the dealer to update the program.

Growing the system. Both can start with one room and expand. If you expect to add lighting control, shades and climate across the house over time, say so at the start, because it affects which processor and platform we recommend.

Homes and businesses. We specify both in residences. In offices, meeting rooms and hospitality spaces, RTI’s custom interfaces are often the better match for staff who need two or three clearly labelled buttons.

How we help clients choose

  • Whole-home automation with lighting and many rooms? Control4 is usually the natural fit.
  • Media rooms, a main living area or a boardroom? RTI often gives the cleaner result.
  • An existing system in the house? It is usually better to service and extend what is there than to replace it. We take over existing RTI systems and support Control4 systems installed by others.
  • Guests and staff using the system? Both can be made simple. Control4 is consistent by default; RTI is as simple as it is designed to be.

What matters more than the brand

Three things decide whether you enjoy the system five years from now. The network, because most control problems start there. The programming, because that is what you touch every day. And local support, because equipment changes and things need adjusting. Our comparison of professional and DIY smart homes covers the same ground from another angle.

Common questions

Can I switch from one to the other?

Yes. Speakers, displays, wiring and often lighting hardware stay; the processor, remotes and programming change.

Do both work with Sonos and Lutron?

Yes. Both integrate with Sonos audio and Lutron lighting and shades.

Which is easier to get serviced in the Bahamas?

Whichever has an authorized dealer on island who answers the phone. Island Integration is a dealer for both.

Not sure which system fits?

We install and program both Control4 and RTI, and will recommend the one that suits your property.
