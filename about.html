Our story

Island Integration began with a simple idea: technology should disappear into beautiful spaces.

Over the last decade we’ve helped homeowners, developers and hospitality teams create environments that feel effortless: reliable networks, intuitive control and cinematic AV without visual clutter.

We work across Nassau and New Providence, including Paradise Island, Lyford Cay, Old Fort Bay, Albany and Ocean Club, and tailor every system to the property, with craftsmanship that lasts.

What we believe

  • Design first. Technology should enhance architecture, not fight it.
  • Reliability matters. Enterprise-grade networks are the backbone of every system.
  • One-touch living. Scenes simplify complex spaces into simple experiences.
  • Long-term support. We maintain, monitor and evolve systems as needs change.
