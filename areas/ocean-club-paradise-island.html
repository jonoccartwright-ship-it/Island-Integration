Ocean Club &amp; Paradise Island

Smart home installation for Ocean Club and Paradise Island residences

Condominiums, villas and vacation residences have their own demands: compact equipment, wireless-friendly design, remote management and systems that guests can use without instructions. We have installed and supported systems at Ocean Club and across Paradise Island.

How we work in Ocean Club and Paradise Island

Paradise Island residences are often second or third homes, used for weeks at a time and rented or lent to guests in between. The technology has to be simple on arrival, robust when nobody is there, and manageable from abroad. Condominium buildings add their own constraints: shared infrastructure, limited equipment space, strata rules about what can be installed and no attic to run cable through.

We design compact, reliable systems for these properties, from Control4 automation and professional Wi-Fi to TV and audio, motorized shades, climate and smart locks and cameras. Our Ocean Club Condos project shows a typical installation: a Control4 Core 3 controller, Araknis networking and OvrC remote management.

What we deliver here

  • Compact automation. Control4 systems sized for apartments and villas, with one remote per room and the app for owners.
  • Reliable Wi-Fi in concrete buildings. Wired access points placed for coverage through block walls and across balconies.
  • Guest-friendly entertainment. TVs and audio that work on the first press, with streaming services ready to go.
  • Shades and climate for empty periods. Schedules that protect finishes from sun and keep humidity in a safe range while the unit is unoccupied.
  • Smart locks and cameras. Codes for guests, cleaners and managers, and interior or entry cameras where appropriate.
  • Remote management. OvrC-based monitoring so we can fix most issues without entering the property.

How we work

From first call to handover

  1. Respect the building

    We review condominium rules and existing infrastructure, work with building management on access and scheduling, and keep installations tidy and reversible where required.

  2. Design for guests and managers

    Simple interfaces for visitors, fuller control for owners and a manager level for the people who look after the unit.

  3. Install quickly and cleanly

    Compact racks, surface-mounted solutions where walls cannot be opened and minimal disruption to neighbours.

Local knowledge

Good to know about Ocean Club and Paradise Island

  • Rental and guest use. If the property is rented, we set up guest profiles and codes that can be changed between stays, and keep owner settings protected.
  • Humidity when closed up. A sealed, unoccupied unit in the tropics needs humidity management. Remote climate visibility is one of the most valued features for owners abroad. See smart climate control.
  • Shared internet. Buildings sometimes provide internet; we design the in-unit network to work well behind it, or arrange a dedicated connection where performance matters.

Frequently asked questions

Can you install in a condo without running new cable?

Usually. Wireless lighting and shade motors, a single well-placed wired access point and compact equipment make most apartments achievable with little or no new cabling.

Can a property manager control the system?

Yes. Managers can be given their own access to climate, locks and cameras, separate from the owner's.

Do you cover other parts of Paradise Island?

Yes. We serve residences and villas across Paradise Island as well as Ocean Club.
