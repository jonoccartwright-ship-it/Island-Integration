Smart home automation, networking and AV for Lyford Cay homes

How we work in Lyford Cay

Lyford Cay homes tend to be large, long-established and frequently renovated. Many have been extended over decades, so the technology inside is often a patchwork: an older alarm, a few Wi-Fi extenders, several remotes and lighting that only works from the wall. Our work in the community is usually about bringing all of that into one coherent, reliable system without disturbing the finishes or the way the house is lived in.

We design and install Control4 automation, lighting control, estate-grade networking, cinema and media rooms and security and access control for Lyford Cay residences, and we support them remotely so owners who are abroad for part of the year always have a local team watching the system.

What we deliver here

  • Whole-property automation. Main house, guest cottages, pool and dock on one system, with scenes for arrival, entertaining and away periods.
  • Estate Wi-Fi. Wired access points across the house and grounds, with fiber between buildings and coverage to the waterfront.
  • Renovation-friendly lighting control. Wireless Lutron and Control4 dimmers where walls cannot be opened; centralized panels where they can.
  • Security that respects privacy. Cameras on approaches and perimeters, video intercom at gates, and access codes for staff and contractors.
  • Entertainment. Media rooms, outdoor audio and distributed video sized to large open-plan living spaces.
  • Absentee-owner support. Remote monitoring and coordination with property managers and house staff.

How we work

From first call to handover

  1. Survey the whole property

    We walk the main house, outbuildings and grounds to understand existing wiring, construction and where coverage and control are needed, and we talk to the staff who run the house day to day.

  2. Phase around how you use the house

    Many Lyford Cay projects are phased: network and security first, then lighting and entertainment during a renovation. We design the full system up front so each phase fits the next.

  3. Work alongside your builder and designer

    Renovations in the community typically involve architects, interior designers and contractors. We coordinate device locations, finishes and schedules so technology is part of the plan rather than an afterthought.

  4. Hand over and keep watching

    After commissioning we walk owners and staff through the system, then monitor it remotely so issues are usually fixed before anyone notices.

Local knowledge

Good to know about Lyford Cay

  • Older construction. Thick walls and earlier renovations make wireless coverage and new cabling harder. We rely on wired access points and discreet cable paths, and we photograph everything before walls close.
  • Waterfront exposure. Salt air and sea spray are hard on exterior cameras, speakers and access points. We specify marine-grade equipment and seal every connection. See protecting electronics from salt air.
  • Second homes. Many Lyford Cay owners split their time between countries. Remote climate and humidity control, camera access and smart locks let them manage the house from anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Can you upgrade an existing system rather than replace it?

Often, yes. We assess what is installed, keep what is sound and integrate it where possible. Older alarm panels, speakers and cabling can frequently be reused within a new control system.

Do you work with house managers and staff?

Yes. We train staff on the system, provide them with the level of control they need, and act as the technical contact for property managers when owners are away.

How quickly can you respond to a problem?

Most issues are diagnosed and resolved remotely the same day. When a visit is needed, we are based in Nassau and schedule promptly.
